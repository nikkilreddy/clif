================================================================================
DEMO CONCEPT & FLOW
================================================================================
- We act as the Threat Actor targeting SecureBank (our realistic banking target).
- SecureBank emits live security events as the attack script runs.
- CLIF captures and streams logs in real-time through the high-speed data flow path:
  SecureBank -> Vector (Rust) -> Redpanda (Stream Broker) -> Consumer-Go (LZ4 + UUID5) -> ClickHouse
- The 3-Agent AI Pipeline (Triage -> Hunter -> Verifier) scores, investigates, correlates the Attack Graph, and outputs court-admissible forensic verdicts.

WORD-FOR-WORD SCRIPT FOR RECORDING
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM & THE VISION (00:00 – 00:30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Open Dashboard Home at http://localhost:3001
- Show the clean dark-mode SOC interface with active agent statuses and live telemetry counters.

SPEAK:
"Every single day, Security Operations Centers receive over 10,000 security alerts. Over 45% of them are false alarms. Human analysts drown in noise, while real cyberattacks take months to detect.

Legacy SIEMs rely on rigid IF-ELSE rules that break under novel mutations, while generic LLM wrappers are too slow and hallucinate.

We built CLIF (Cognitive Log Investigation Platform)—an autonomous 3-agent SIEM system that ingests raw logs at line rate, investigates cross-host attack kill chains, and delivers court-admissible forensic verdicts in under 60 seconds."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 2: INGESTION & LIVE ATTACK SIMULATION (00:30 – 01:05)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Open SecureBank at http://localhost:5001
- Open the Live Feed at http://localhost:3001/live-feed
- Open a split terminal alongside the browser and run:
  python3 demo/securebank/attack.py --fast

SPEAK:
"To demonstrate this live, we act as the threat actor targeting our vulnerable banking application, SecureBank.

In our terminal, we launch a real multi-stage attack: port reconnaissance, credential brute-forcing, SQL injection, and database exfiltration.

Notice what happens on our Live Feed: SecureBank is continuously emitting raw security events.

CLIF captures this firehose instantly through our high-speed data flow path: from Rust-native Vector, into Redpanda's distributed stream, batched via our custom Go consumer with LZ4 compression and UUID5 deduplication, and stored directly into ClickHouse at over 35,000 events per second with zero data loss."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 3: TRIAGE ML & ATTACK GRAPH (01:05 – 01:45)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Navigate to Investigations (http://localhost:3001/investigations)
- Open the escalated investigation and zoom into the interactive Attack Graph.

SPEAK:
"Notice what happens under the hood:

First, our Triage Agent extracts 60 numerical features in microseconds and runs an ONNX dual-model ensemble—combining supervised LightGBM with an unsupervised Deep Neural Autoencoder to catch both known attacks and zero-day anomalies.

When an event is flagged, it triggers our Hunter Agent. Rather than treating alerts in isolation, Hunter queries ClickHouse for surrounding temporal context across the entire network and dynamically constructs this Attack Graph—visually connecting the attacker's external IP, the compromised user account, and the target database table."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 4: VERIFIER VERDICT & NARRATIVE (01:45 – 02:20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Scroll down to the Verifier Verdict & Forensic Report section.
- Highlight the Verdict Badge, Confidence Score, Priority Level, and Explainable AI (XAI) metrics.

SPEAK:
"Finally, our Verifier Agent acts like a Senior Forensic Investigator. It evaluates evidence coherence and produces a calibrated verdict: True Positive, Priority 1 Critical, with a 94% confidence score.

Crucially, if evidence is contradictory or missing, the system gracefully degrades and issues an explicit Inconclusive verdict instead of hallucinating dangerous automated actions.

It also generates this plain-English forensic narrative and step-by-step timeline, ready for human analysts or courtroom submission."
