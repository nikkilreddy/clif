📍 SCENE 1: THE TARGET PERSONA & THE PAIN (00:00 – 00:35)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ON SCREEN]: 
- Open Dashboard Home at http://localhost:3001
- Show the dark-mode SOC dashboard with active agent telemetry cards and metrics.

[SPEAK]:
"Hey everyone! 

If you walk into any enterprise today and talk to a Tier-1 or Tier-2 SOC Incident Responder, they are literally drowning. They face over 10,000 alerts every single shift, and more than 45% are noisy false positives. When a real attack happens, manually piecing together fragmented logs takes hours.

Traditional SIEMs rely on brittle IF-ELSE playbooks that break the second an attacker tweaks a script. And simply throwing logs into a single ChatGPT prompt is too slow, expensive, and hallucinates fake CVEs.

So, we built CLIF—the Cognitive Log Investigation Platform. 

CLIF replaces manual playbook writing with a self-orchestrating 3-agent pipeline—Triage, Hunter, and Verifier—that takes millions of raw logs and delivers verified forensic verdicts in under sixty seconds."

📍 SCENE 2: THE TARGET ATTACK & HIGH-SPEED DATA PIPELINE (00:35 – 01:10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ON SCREEN]: 
- Show SecureBank at http://localhost:5001 (our target banking application).
- Show the Live Feed at http://localhost:3001/live-feed.
- Open a split terminal on the right and run:
  python3 demo/securebank/attack.py --fast

[SPEAK]:
"Let’s see it live. We’re putting on our attacker hat and targeting SecureBank, our vulnerable banking application.

In the terminal on the right, I'm firing off a multi-stage attack: port scanning, brute-forcing admin credentials, and executing SQL injection.

Over on our Live Feed, SecureBank is pumping out security logs. 

Now, here was our first non-obvious engineering challenge: how do you stream and store logs at line rate with zero data loss? 

We didn't use an external API—we built custom Rust Vector transforms to canonicalize logs, buffered them in a Redpanda stream, and wrote a custom Go consumer utilizing zero-allocation buffers, LZ4 compression, and deterministic UUID5 deduplication to batch-insert into ClickHouse at over 35,000 events per second."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 3: THE TWO-MODEL ENSEMBLE & HUNTER ATTACK GRAPH (01:10 – 01:50)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ON SCREEN]: 
- Navigate to Investigations at http://localhost:3001/investigations
- Click the escalated investigation and zoom into the interactive Attack Graph.

[SPEAK]:
"Now, let's look at the AI brain. We don't rely on a single model—we use a cooperating two-model ensemble.

First, our Triage Agent extracts 60 numerical features across seven domain layers in just ten microseconds. It runs two models in parallel:
- Model 1 is a supervised LightGBM ONNX classifier for known attack patterns.
- Model 2 is an unsupervised Deep Neural Autoencoder for zero-day behavioral anomalies.

Here’s the cool part: What if one model fails? If an attacker uses a stealthy zero-day that LightGBM scores as zero, the Autoencoder catches the abnormal reconstruction error at 0.92 and escalates it anyway.

That triggers our Hunter Agent. Instead of treating alerts in isolation, Hunter runs five correlation engines—including Sigma rules and 3-sigma statistical anomalies—to dynamically construct this Attack Graph you see right here. It visually links the attacker's external IP, shows how they compromised the admin account, and pinpointed the exact database table they exfiltrated."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 4: VERIFIER FORENSIC VERDICT & HANDLING BEING WRONG (01:50 – 02:25)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ON SCREEN]: 
- Scroll down to the Verifier section.
- Point out the Verdict Badge (True Positive), Confidence (94%), and the plain-English timeline.

[SPEAK]:
"Next, our Verifier Agent steps in as the Senior Forensic Investigator. It checks evidence coherence across the entire graph and gives us a calibrated verdict: True Positive, Priority 1 Critical, with 94% confidence.

Now, what if the model is wrong or data is missing? 

We built in strict Graceful Degradation: if logs are conflicting or incomplete, the system will NEVER guess or trigger an unsafe automated action. It explicitly flags the incident as 'Inconclusive' with wider confidence bounds.

Plus, it automatically generates this plain-English forensic narrative and step-by-step timeline, ready for human analysts or courtroom submission."
