================================================================================
DEMO CONCEPT AT A GLANCE
================================================================================
- We act as the hacker attacking SecureBank (our demo banking app).
- SecureBank generates live security logs as the attack runs.
- CLIF captures and streams the logs in real time:
  SecureBank -> Vector (Rust) -> Redpanda -> Consumer-Go -> ClickHouse
- Our 3 AI Agents (Triage -> Hunter -> Verifier) automatically catch the attack, connect the dots on a visual graph, and explain what happened in plain English.

WORD-FOR-WORD CONVERSATIONAL SCRIPT
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM & THE IDEA (00:00 – 00:30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Open Dashboard Home at http://localhost:3001
- Show the clean dark-mode dashboard with live metrics.

SPEAK:
"Hey everyone! 

If you talk to any security analyst today, they'll tell you the exact same headache: they get flooded with over 10,000 alerts every single day, and nearly half of them are complete false alarms. Real attacks get buried, and digging through logs takes hours.

Old-school tools rely on rigid if-else rules that break easily, and simply tossing raw logs into ChatGPT is way too slow and makes things up.

So, we built CLIF. It's an autonomous multi-agent platform that catches threats in real time, connects the dots across your entire network, and gives you a clear, verified answer in under a minute."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 2: ATTACKING SECUREBANK & LIVE STREAMING (00:30 – 01:05)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Open SecureBank at http://localhost:5001
- Open the Live Feed at http://localhost:3001/live-feed
- Open a split terminal alongside the browser and run:
  python3 demo/securebank/attack.py --fast

SPEAK:
"Let’s see it in action. Here on screen, we have SecureBank—our demo banking app. 

We’re going to put on our hacker hat and attack it right now. In the terminal on the right, I'm firing off our attack script—running port scans, brute-forcing the login, and hitting it with a SQL injection.

Over on our Live Feed, you can see SecureBank pumping out security logs in real time. 

CLIF captures all of this instantly. We built a super-fast pipeline using Vector in Rust, a Redpanda stream, and a Go consumer that batches and compresses everything into ClickHouse at over 35,000 events a second without dropping a single packet."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 3: THE AI AGENTS & ATTACK GRAPH (01:05 – 01:45)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Navigate to Investigations (http://localhost:3001/investigations)
- Click on the new investigation and zoom into the interactive Attack Graph.

SPEAK:
"Now, here’s where the AI magic happens. 

First, our Triage Agent takes every single log, extracts 60 key features in microseconds, and runs them through our ML ensemble—using LightGBM for known attack patterns and a Neural Autoencoder for weird zero-day anomalies.

When something looks suspicious, it hands it over to our Hunter Agent. 

Instead of looking at alerts in isolation, Hunter digs into ClickHouse, checks surrounding activity across the whole network, and automatically builds this Attack Graph you see right here. It connects the attacker’s external IP, shows how they broke into the admin account, and pinpoints the exact database table they touched."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📍 SCENE 4: VERIFIER VERDICT & PLAIN-ENGLISH TIMELINE (01:45 – 02:20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON SCREEN:
- Scroll down to the Verifier section.
- Point out the Verdict Badge (True Positive), Confidence (94%), and the plain-English timeline.

SPEAK:
"Finally, our Verifier Agent steps in like a senior investigator. It looks at the whole picture and gives us a clear verdict: True Positive, Priority 1 Critical, with 94% confidence.

And here's the best part: if the logs are incomplete or contradictory, it doesn't hallucinate or make risky guesses—it safely flags it as 'Inconclusive'.

Plus, it writes out this clean timeline explaining in plain English exactly what happened, along with the next steps to fix it, ready for the security team or even a courtroom."
